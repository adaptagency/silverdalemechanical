Motorhome Servicing

Motorhome Servicing, Auckland
Done Right

Silverdale Mechanical Engineering is a full-service mechanical workshop in Silverdale, North Auckland, with specialist understanding of large diesel drivetrains. We keep motorhomes, camper vans, buses and coaches safe, reliable and ready for the road — petrol and diesel, all makes.

Our motorhome servicing covers diesel drivetrain servicing, chassis and suspension, brake system overhauls and pre-trip safety inspections — always to manufacturer specifications, using quality Caltex oils and lubricants. We also help you stay on the right side of compliance with WOF and COF checks.

As an MTA Assured member with over 45 years of experience, we understand the unique demands of large recreational and commercial coaches — so your trips stay on schedule and on budget.

Which Motorhomes & Coaches
Do We Service?

Our workshop services petrol and diesel vehicles of all makes, with a specialism in large diesel drivetrains. We routinely service and maintain:
