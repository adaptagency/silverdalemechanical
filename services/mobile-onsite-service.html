Mobile On-Site Service

On-Site Mechanical Servicing,
Hibiscus Coast & Auckland Wide

Silverdale Mechanical Engineering operates a fully equipped mobile service unit that brings the mechanical workshop to you — to your worksite, your depot, your yard, or the roadside. When a vehicle or machine can't be safely or economically transported, our mobile mechanics across Auckland & the Hibiscus Coast come to it instead.

Our mobile unit is ideal for remote sites and fleet operators, and for handling emergency breakdowns where towing simply isn't practical. It carries a full tool inventory and its own generator equipment on board, so our mechanics can complete most servicing and repairs on site — quickly and with minimal disruption to your operations.

We regularly perform on-site servicing for plant machinery such as excavators and heavy equipment at construction and quarry sites across Auckland & the Hibiscus Coast — including Orewa, Millwater, Milldale, Red Beach, Whangaparaoa and Dairy Flat — along with scheduled fleet servicing and roadside emergency response. It's the same MTA Assured workmanship as our Silverdale workshop — just delivered where you need it.

Who It's For

Who Needs a Mobile Mechanic
in Auckland?

Fleet operators use our mobile service to keep trucks, vans and utility vehicles serviced without pulling them off the road for a day. We perform scheduled maintenance at your depot, around your schedule, minimising downtime across the whole fleet.

Construction and earthmoving sites rely on us for on-site servicing and repairs of excavators, diggers and heavy plant — machines that are expensive and difficult to transport. We come to the worksite to keep the job moving.

Anyone stuck on the roadside benefits from our emergency response. For motorhomes, trucks, and heavy vehicles that can't easily be towed, a mobile mechanic can often get you moving again on the spot.
