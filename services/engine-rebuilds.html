Is a Rebuild Right for You?

Does My Engine Need
a Rebuild?

Diesel engines give plenty of warning when they need major attention. Common signs include a noticeable loss of power, excessive smoke, oil consumption, unusual knocking or rattling, hard starting, or a cooling system that keeps overheating. If you are seeing any of these and are unsure whether a repair or a rebuild is the right call, our mechanics can assess the engine and give you straight, honest advice.

We work on all engine sizes and configurations — from the six-cylinder diesels found in NZ trucks and buses to the heavy plant engines fitted to excavators and machinery. Our rebuild process is methodical: strip, inspect and measure, replace what is worn, reassemble to specification, and test before the engine goes back to work.

Keeping your original engine running with a quality rebuild can be a far better investment than replacing the vehicle — especially when the rest of the vehicle and its driveline are still in good condition.

Common Rebuild Symptoms

  • Loss of Power or Misfiring
  • Excessive Smoke or Oil Use
  • Knocking, Rattling or Noisy Valves
  • Hard Starting & Overheating
  • High Mileage & Wear
  • Post-Accident or Post-Failure Damage
