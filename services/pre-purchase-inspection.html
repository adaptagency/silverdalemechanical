Know Before You Buy

What We Inspect

What Does Our Used Car
Inspection Cover?

Our inspectors work through the whole vehicle, system by system, so nothing is left to chance. We assess the engine, drivetrain, brakes and suspension, along with hydraulic and electrical systems — critical on motorhomes, trucks and plant machinery.

We also carry out a structural and chassis inspection to identify damage, corrosion or previous repairs, and review the service history so you can see how the vehicle has been maintained.

Whatever you are buying — a used car, a truck, a motorhome or a piece of machinery — our pre-purchase inspection in Silverdale gives you the full picture, backed by a written report.

  • Brakes & Suspension
  • Hydraulic Systems

  • Chassis & Structure
  • Service History & Documentation
