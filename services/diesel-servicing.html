Diesel Servicing

Diesel Servicing, Auckland
Done Right

Silverdale Mechanical Engineering is a full-service mechanical workshop in Silverdale, North Auckland, specialising in diesel-powered vehicles of every size — from fleet utes to heavy trucks, motorhomes, buses, coaches and plant machinery.

Our diesel mechanics carry out comprehensive servicing to manufacturer specifications, covering everything from routine oil and filter changes through to engine top tunes, clutch and flywheel service, belt replacement and full engine rebuilds. Every job uses top-quality Caltex oils and lubricants to keep your engine protected.

As an MTA Assured member and authorised Kobelco and Attach2 agent, we bring genuine manufacturer-level expertise to every diesel engine we touch — in our workshop at 14 Anvil Road, Silverdale, or on-site through our fully equipped mobile service unit.

Vehicles We Service

Which Diesel Vehicles
Do We Service?

Our workshop handles petrol and diesel vehicles of all makes, with a true specialism in diesel-powered work vehicles. We routinely service and maintain:

Heavy trucks, transport units and commercial fleet vehicles — keeping scheduled maintenance programmes on track to minimise downtime; utes and 4WDs, the workhorses of New Zealand — including Toyota Hilux and common NZ utes; motorhomes, camper vans, buses and coaches, with specialist understanding of large diesel drivetrains; and excavators and plant machinery as authorised Kobelco service agents.

Whether you run a single ute or a whole fleet, our diesel servicing in Silverdale is designed to keep your vehicles reliable, compliant and ready to work hard.
