What We Do

A Full-Service Mechanical Workshop
in Silverdale, Auckland

Silverdale Mechanical Engineering has been North Auckland's trusted heavy vehicle and machinery specialist for over 45 years. As an MTA Assured workshop, authorised Kobelco service agent and authorised Attach2 agent, we service everything from cars and SUVs to heavy trucks, motorhomes, excavators and fleet vehicles — using top-quality Caltex oils and lubricants on every job.
