Your Local Mechanic

Whangaparaoa's
Local Heavy-Vehicle Mechanic

If you're after a mechanic in Whangaparaoa, Silverdale Mechanical Engineering is the Peninsula's natural home base for serious mechanical work. We're at 14 Anvil Road, Silverdale — a 10–15 minute run up from Whangaparaoa Road — and locals from Stanmore Bay to Gulf Harbour make the trip for work their regular garages can't handle.

That work starts where general service stops: diesel servicing, engine rebuilds, COF and WOF pre-checks and motorhome and coach servicing. Peninsula motorhomers and boat-towing 4WDs get looked after too, plus hydraulic systems, plant and machinery, and pre-purchase inspections.

Don't want to head off the peninsula? Our fully equipped mobile on-site service unit does the rounds of Whangaparaoa and the Hibiscus Coast — depots, marina carparks, building sites and the roadside. Same MTA Assured workmanship, none of the towing.

Silverdale Mechanical is MTA Assured, an authorised Kobelco and Attach2 agent, and a Caltex fuel and lubricants partner — the trust and accreditation Whangaparaoa deserves from its local mechanic.

Living at the end of the peninsula shouldn't mean travelling into town for decent service. We're close enough that Whangaparaoa families drop off for a WOF on the way to the shops, and our mobile unit means businesses and boaties on the peninsula never have to tow anything anywhere. Many of our Whangaparaoa customers have been coming to us for years — the sort of relationship trust is built on. Whether you drive a commuter, run a van around the peninsula or keep a motorhome ready for the next trip away, there's a strong chance we've already worked on something just like it.
