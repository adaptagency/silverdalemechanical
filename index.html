About Us

We Love Getting

Silverdale Mechanical Engineering has been North Auckland's most trusted heavy vehicle specialist for over four decades. From comprehensive diesel engine rebuilds to mobile on-site emergency repairs — we bring professional-grade expertise directly to you.

We are MTA Assured, authorised Kobelco service agents, and authorised Attach2 agents. We use top-quality Caltex oils and lubricants on every job.

What We Do

Our Services

Comprehensive mechanical solutions for every heavy vehicle requirement

Diesel Servicing

Comprehensive diesel engine servicing to manufacturer specifications. Oil & filter changes, top tunes, clutch servicing, belt replacement and engine rebuilds using quality Caltex lubricants.

  • Scheduled Oil & Filter Changes
  • Engine Top Tunes
  • Brake Relining & ABS Diagnostics
  • Clutch & Flywheel Service
  • Full Engine Rebuilds

Plant & Machinery

On-site and workshop servicing for all plant equipment including excavators, bulldozers and heavy machinery. We come to your worksite — minimising costly downtime.

  • Scheduled On/Off-Site Servicing
  • Hydraulic System Maintenance
  • Track Adjustments & Greasing
  • On-Site Repairs & Welding
  • Pre-Purchase Inspections

Engineering & Welding

Full fabrication, welding and engineering services in our workshop or on your site. Our mobile unit carries welding gear and a generator for rapid field repairs.

  • MIG, TIG & Arc Welding
  • Chassis Rust Repairs
  • Custom Fabrication
  • On-Site Emergency Welding
  • Plant Equipment Modifications

COF & WOF Pre-Checks

Thorough pre-inspection checks to ensure your vehicles pass their Certificate of Fitness or Warrant of Fitness first time. We identify and rectify issues before the official inspection.

  • Certificate of Fitness (COF)
  • Warrant of Fitness (WOF)
  • Brake System Inspection
  • Safety Systems Check
  • Compliance Documentation

Mobile On-Site Service

Our fully equipped mobile service unit brings the workshop to you. Ideal for remote sites, fleet operators and emergency breakdowns where transporting machinery is not practical.

  • Roadside Emergency Response
  • On-Site Fleet Servicing
  • Remote Location Service
  • Generator & Welding Equipment
  • Full Tool Inventory On-Board

Motorhome Servicing

Specialist motorhome and coach mechanical servicing. From diesel drivetrains to chassis maintenance — we understand the unique demands of large recreational and commercial coaches.

  • Diesel Drivetrain Servicing
  • Chassis & Suspension
  • Brake System Overhaul
  • Pre-Trip Safety Inspections
  • WOF / COF Compliance

Hydraulic Systems

Expert hydraulic system servicing and repair. We maintain and overhaul hydraulic rams, hoses, cylinders and pumps to keep your plant running reliably under pressure.

  • Hydraulic Oil & Filter Service
  • Ram Replacement & Repair
  • Hose & Fitting Replacement
  • Pump & Valve Overhaul
  • System Pressure Testing

Engine Rebuilds

Full diesel engine rebuilds and major overhauls. Whether it is a top-end rebuild or complete engine replacement, our experienced technicians restore power and reliability.

  • Top-End & Full Rebuilds
  • Valve & Injector Overhaul
  • Timing & Belt Replacement
  • Turbocharger Service
  • Post-Rebuild Dyno Testing

Pre-Purchase Inspection

Thinking of buying a truck, motorhome or piece of machinery? Our thorough pre-purchase inspection gives you the full picture before you commit — so you are not caught out.

  • Full Mechanical Assessment
  • Hydraulic & Electrical Check
  • Structural & Chassis Inspection
  • Service History Review
  • Written Report Provided
