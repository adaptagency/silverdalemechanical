## About the Business

Silverdale Mechanical Engineering Limited is a full-service mechanical workshop based in Silverdale, Auckland. The business serves a broad range of customers — from everyday car and SUV owners to operators of heavy trucks, plant machinery, and commercial fleets. Services cover everything from routine servicing through to engine rebuilds, hydraulic systems, mobile on-site work, and compliance checks.

The workshop is backed by authorised partnerships with MTA Assured, Kobelco, and Attach2, and is supported by Caltex oils and lubricants. Workshop management is powered by AutoHive.

---

## Why This Website Exists

The website was built to replace an older, less polished online presence and to give Silverdale Mechanical a digital front door that properly reflects the quality and breadth of the workshop.

**The site has four primary jobs:**

1. **Attract new customers** — position the workshop as the trusted, premium choice for car and truck mechanical work in the Auckland region.
2. **Convert visitors into enquiries** — make it fast and easy for people to request a free quote or book a service.
3. **Retain existing customers** — give returning customers a familiar, low-friction path to book their next service.
4. **Build credibility** — use strong branding, clear service information, and social proof (partner logos, stat counters, process steps) to earn trust before the first contact.

The website serves two distinct but overlapping audiences:

| Audience | What they're looking for |
|---|---|
| **Car & SUV owners** | A reliable, local mechanic they can trust. Fast quote. Easy booking. |

## What the Site Covers

The website is structured as a single, scrollable page with 12 sections:

- **Fixed navigation** — always accessible, two clear calls to action: Free Quote and Book a Service
- **Hero** — full-screen first impression with workshop positioning and a primary CTA
- **Service marquee** — scrolling strip of key services for quick scanning
- **About the workshop** — who Silverdale Mechanical are and why they stand out
- **Services grid** — all 10 services presented as easy-to-scan cards
- **Vehicle types** — seven tiles showing the full range of vehicles the workshop handles
- **Parallax quote** — editorial credibility moment (Henry Ford)
- **Why Choose Us** — animated statistics that build trust at a glance
- **How It Works** — four-step process that makes engaging feel simple
- **Booking section** — two-tab form (Free Quote / Book a Service) with date picker
- **Contact section** — phone, email, address, hours, directions, and embedded map
- **Footer** — partners, legal links, and contact details

## Design & Feel

The site uses a **dark premium industrial** aesthetic — appropriate for a trade workshop that wants to be seen as professional and capable, not rough around the edges.

- **Primary colour:** Deep crimson red — used for CTAs and highlights to draw the eye
- **Background:** Near-black — creates a cinematic, high-contrast feel
- **Typography:** Bold condensed headlines (Bebas Neue), clean body text (Inter)
- **Imagery:** Full-screen, full-bleed background images — no thin dividers, no cluttered layouts
- **Tone:** Capable, confident, direct — speaks to people who need work done, not people browsing casually

---

## Booking System

The site includes an active booking section with two separate forms:

**Free Quote** — for new customers who just want a price. Simple, fast, no commitment.

**Book a Service** — for returning customers who know what they want. Includes vehicle registration and workshop/mobile preference.

Both forms use a native browser date picker (no clunky pop-up calendars), include clear inline validation, and show a success confirmation on submission.

> **Note on form submission:** The forms are currently set up to receive submissions on the front end. EmailJS integration is the next step to deliver them to the workshop inbox.
